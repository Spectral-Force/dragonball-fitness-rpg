## Unlock contract

The story engine evaluates only the exact saga requested.

- `entry`: the saga is actually unlocked.
- `development` and `preclimax`: the saga is unlocked and its Focus ratio meets the authored threshold.
- `resolution`: the saga is actually cleared.
- `mastery`: the saga is actually mastered.
- Character beats require the exact owned partner, partner level and saga status in their trigger.
- Relationship beats require both exact partners, both levels and the exact saga status.

Locked or similarly named sagas cannot leak state. A saved legacy story receipt is historical metadata, not authorization for resolution or mastery.
